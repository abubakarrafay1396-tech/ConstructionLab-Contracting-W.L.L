Who we are

From a single unit to a larger project.

We are an air-conditioning and ductwork company in Bahrain, with 15+ years in HVAC: heating, ventilation and air conditioning.

Our work ranges from single split unit installations to large commercial and public projects, including schools and hospitals. We offer turnkey projects, bringing the work together from design through installation and commissioning.
